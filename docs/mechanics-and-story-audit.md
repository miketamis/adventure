# Mechanics and story audit

This audit treats mechanics as reusable verbs and state systems. A story-specific object is an expression of a mechanic, not a new mechanic by itself. In particular, **“a persistent campfire” is not a mechanic**. The mechanic is a **timed world fixture**: a place-bound thing is activated, persists between visits, changes through authored stages as its owning clock advances, and can alter prose or choices. The clearing’s fire and Maro’s mill lamp are its first two expressions.

The governing rule is fit: use every mechanic the lore naturally supports, but never distort a source tale merely to tick a systems box.

## Current mechanic inventory

### Learning and choice

- Token-level Albanian discovery, meaning, inflected-form display and audio.
- Reviewed whole-sentence readings and action phrases.
- Context, form, word and full-phrase practice, weighted toward useful and weak material.
- Mana earned through comprehension and spent by speaking choices.
- Reveal-gated paths and plausible-but-impossible confuser actions.
- Persistent vocabulary and practice progress across runs.

### Character and resources

- Hearts, injuries, story-described health, self-healing and paid or item healing.
- Inventory acquisition, requirements, consumption and direct item use.
- Companions that travel and participate in conditional outcomes.
- Lek earned through work and spent on food, lodging, trade and care.
- Embodied roles with confirmation, identity, isolated health and inventory, pausing, public-world detours and exact resumption.

### Living world

- A spatial mythic chart with exact routes, distance, travel time, direction and backtracking.
- Arrival-aware prose, phase-crossing prose and leaner revisit text.
- Rumours that mark unheard places and pay off on first arrival.
- Day phases, deterministic calendar, weekdays, seasons, weather, hydrology and data-driven observances.
- Long-journey interstitials with sourced stages rather than silent clock jumps.
- Timed world fixtures with reusable stages and groups; currently the clearing fire and mill lamp.
- Clock-walking NPC schedules and one-shot processions.
- Lasting world facts with ambient presentation and incompatible-outcome reconciliation.
- Map sightlines, weather and season visibility, mini-map, atlas and keyboard navigation.

### Story, lore and persistence

- Branching good, secret and bad outcomes; failure still produces learning and remembered fates.
- Achievement deeds followed by strict comprehension gates.
- A 140-card sourced lore codex with relationships.
- Forty-seven full tale records with witnesses, beat timelines, cast, place mirrors, discrepancies and playable projections; one is deliberately source-only.
- Save migration and adversarial replay protection.

The remaining 93 lore cards are supporting mythology, beings, customs, places and history rather than omitted quest scripts. They participate through the codex, relationships, definitions, source links, world prose and achievement explanations. They should become full storylines only when a selected witness and a genuinely playable, lore-fitting action arc exist.

## Enforcement

`node scripts/mechanicsaudit.mjs` fails when a full tale loses its explicit review, when a fixture becomes an unregistered one-off, or when the upgrades above lose their health, item or world-state contracts. It is part of `npm run audit:experience`.
