# Mechanics and story audit

This audit treats mechanics as reusable verbs and state systems. A story-specific object is an expression of a mechanic, not a new mechanic by itself. In particular, **“a persistent campfire” is not a mechanic**. The mechanic is a **timed world fixture**: a place-bound thing is activated, persists between visits, changes through authored stages as its owning clock advances, and can alter prose or choices. The clearing’s fire and Maro’s mill lamp are its first two expressions.

The governing rule is fit: use every mechanic the lore naturally supports, but never distort a source tale merely to tick a systems box.

## Current mechanic inventory

### Learning and choice

- Token-level Albanian discovery, meaning, inflected-form display and audio.
- Reviewed whole-sentence readings and action phrases.
- Context, form, word and full-phrase practice, weighted toward useful and weak material.
- Mana earned through comprehension and spent by speaking choices.
- Reveal-gated paths and plausible-but-impossible confuser actions.
- Persistent vocabulary and practice progress across runs.
- Durable learned knowledge, kept separate from physical inventory and able to
  unlock later dialogue or actions in another place or run.

### Character and resources

- Hearts, injuries, story-described health, self-healing and paid or item healing.
- Inventory acquisition, requirements, consumption and direct item use.
- Data-driven item kinds, tags and affordances, so a hidden route can ask for
  “a light source” or “an instrument” without hard-coding one object forever.
- Companions that travel and participate in conditional outcomes.
- Lek earned through work and spent on food, lodging, trade and care.
- Named interactions limited by scene, day, tale or run, with optional
  cooldowns; work and performance stay useful without becoming infinite loops.
- Embodied roles with confirmation, identity, isolated health and inventory, pausing, public-world detours and exact resumption.

### Living world

- A spatial mythic chart with exact routes, distance, travel time, direction and backtracking.
- Arrival-aware prose, phase-crossing prose and leaner revisit text.
- Rumours that mark unheard places and pay off on first arrival.
- Day phases, deterministic calendar, weekdays, seasons, weather, hydrology and data-driven observances.
- Civil-clock targets that compose with routes, dates and phases, so midnight,
  dawn and source-exact multi-day journeys land at the hour the prose names.
- Scene-critical weather overrides for events whose storm or rain is part of
  the tale, while ordinary scenes continue to use deterministic live weather.
- Long-journey interstitials with sourced stages rather than silent clock jumps.
- Timed world fixtures with reusable stages, groups and actions (light, relight,
  refuel and extinguish); currently the clearing fire and mill lamp.
- Clock-walking NPC schedules and one-shot processions.
- Lasting world facts with ambient presentation and incompatible-outcome reconciliation.
- Map sightlines, weather and season visibility, mini-map, atlas and keyboard navigation.
- State-responsive map landmarks, including a half-built, completed or absent
  Bridge of Arta instead of one permanent decorative state.

### Story, lore and persistence

- Branching good, secret and bad outcomes; failure still produces learning and remembered fates.
- Typed, composable choice effects for items, resources, flags, knowledge and
  fixtures, with old authored choices adapted through the same transaction path.
- Achievement deeds followed by strict comprehension gates.
- A 140-card sourced lore codex with relationships.
- Forty-seven full tale records with witnesses, beat timelines, cast, place mirrors, discrepancies and playable projections; one is deliberately source-only.
- Save migration and adversarial replay protection.

The important distinction is between a reusable rule and its story-shaped
expression. A campfire and a lamp are fixtures; a torch and a future lantern are
light sources; a mill wage and a song payment are daily interactions. Content
may remain specific and evocative while the engine underneath stays general.
This is what allows rare discoveries to combine conditions—night, remembered
knowledge, a world outcome and an item capability—without creating another
one-off mechanic for every secret.

## Quiet discoveries and callbacks

The world now rewards attention without turning secrets into a checklist.

- A portable light used beneath an old clearing stone at night reveals an
  explicitly authored Zojz trace. The physical mark is run-local, but the
  learned knowledge survives and is recognized much later on Tomorr.
- The cuckoo’s bird-ending can be heard in different woods by day and night;
  the alternate flower-ending leaves a different, incompatible trace.
- The dervish’s victory changes how the trees feel, the defeated coastal Baloz
  changes shore talk, and the Gjakova cavern can retain its fouled water.
- Tomorr’s summit remembers the giants’ battle scars and the Osum below. The
  Blue Eye’s waterwork reaches beyond its source toward the village.
- The hardest coda asks for two completed frontier outcomes and any instrument
  capable of being played. Only then can the traveller add a new paid verse to
  Jutbina’s oda, once per run; returning later changes the room’s response.

These are combinations of reusable capabilities, knowledge, facts, place,
phase and interaction scope. None is promoted as a new standalone mechanic,
and the one invented Zojz trace is labelled as game-world synthesis rather
than a collected local legend.

## Enforcement

`node scripts/mechanicsaudit.mjs` fails when a full tale loses its explicit review, when a fixture becomes an unregistered one-off, or when the upgrades above lose their health, item or world-state contracts. `node scripts/consequencecoherenceaudit.mjs` separately checks that resolved threats stay resolved, permanent facts gain a positive return callback, critical weather reaches the visible scene, and fixtures matter beyond their activation button. Both are part of `npm run audit:experience`.
