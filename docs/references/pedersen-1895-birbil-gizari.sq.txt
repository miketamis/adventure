STATUS
------
The exact Albanian source is TRANSCRIBED AND ALIGNED. The scan's heading is
“Birbil Gizári.” The continuous tale occupies printed pp. 30–35 (roughly
lines 1419–1675 in the Archive OCR). The first excerpts below preserve raw OCR
to make the machine-text defects reproducible. A page-checked, mechanically
transliterated alignment transcript follows them and supplies a source clause
for every one of the game's 154 English sentence-units.

OPENING (printed p. 30; raw OCR)
--------------------------------
UL.
Birbil Gizdri.

AS vie mbrel, ki$ tre djelm, ai mbreti sevdin e tij e kit ne diami,
ne te falur. Asti beri tie dëami Sume te mir, edë si e bilisne ustalarete,
vale mbreti te falej. Atjë ke falej, vate me dervis ë i Va mbretit, ke
ve mire iste dfamija, po anafilë te falte. Si pa mbreti, ke 1 Va
dervisi atë fjale, e prisi diamine nga binaja edë e beri deket me te
mar nga të paren.

The OCR is deliberately not “cleaned” into modern Albanian: symbols such as
$, €, 9 and 18 are recognition errors or font substitutions in the machine
text. It is not used as quotation stock. The alignment transcript below was

Repeated English sentence-units may point to the same clause when the English
