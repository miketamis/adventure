# Player-facing language quality

Last audited: 2026-09-09

This game intentionally keeps three kinds of English separate. They serve
different learning purposes and must never be mistaken for one another:

1. **Token gloss** — the short meaning attached to one Albanian word. It helps
   discovery and vocabulary practice, but its word order is not an English
   sentence.
2. **Reviewed whole-line reading** — natural English written for the complete
   Albanian line, either beside its tokens with `R('…', tokens)` or in the
   address-and-source-pinned deferred reading registry. This is suitable for
   display and assessment.
3. **Aligned fallback reading** — a conservative compatibility reading retained
   as a fail-safe if the optional reading chunk cannot load. Publication
   certification requires that no story line depend on it, and it is never
   accepted into a comprehension test.

Source quotations add two further distinctions. `Q()` preserves the Albanian
quotation and connects it to the [quote proof register](quote-proofs.md). Each
register record carries a `gameTranslation`, a natural-English reading of
exactly the displayed quotation, and a `translation`, the fuller source-passage
or context translation. Only `gameTranslation` can stand in for a reviewed
*whole-line* reading, and only when the registered quotation is the entire
rendered line. When a game line adds a speaker frame, refrain, or other prose,
its authored or deferred reading translates the complete line; the source
translation remains evidence and is never presented as though it translated
only the displayed wording.

assistive voices can switch language at the boundary without changing the
visible learning interaction.

## Learner dictionary definitions

Every saved word has a short Albanian definition in `DEFS`. The same tokenised
definition appears in the Dictionary and, without nested controls, when the
player hovers or focuses that word in learning text. Known definition words use
their Albanian surface and unknown ones retain the normal dashed English gloss;
the tooltip must never create a second discovery or answer-giving interaction.

A definition must identify the exact `DICT` sense. It cannot be a one-word
alias, repeat its own headword, share identical wording with another sense, or
fall back to placeholders such as *një gjë*, *një njeri*, *një vend*, or *një
fjalë e vogël*. Concrete nouns should name a useful category plus a
distinguishing feature or use. Verbs should state a recognizable action or
result. Function words and discourse markers should use a short, natural
micro-example that demonstrates their role. Proper names and lore terms should
locate the person, place, or creature specifically enough to distinguish it
from the rest of the world.

`node scripts/definitionqualityaudit.mjs` enforces structural completeness and
rejects those known placeholder patterns. Semantic and grammatical review must
also compare uncertain senses against an authoritative Albanian explanatory
dictionary, then paraphrase them into original beginner-readable wording; the
release gate is not a substitute for a fluent human reviewer.

`node scripts/dictionaryusageaudit.mjs` separately requires every `DICT` sense
to occur in playable story prose, a player action, an item-use phrase, health
narration, or generated environment narration. Occurrence inside `DEFS` does
not count: a word cannot enter the public dictionary merely to define some
other word and then remain impossible for the player to encounter.

## Inflection paradigms

“Every suffix” means every reviewed grammatical form appropriate to that kind
of word, not a fabricated universal suffix list. Nouns decline for number,
definiteness, and case; verbs, adjectives, pronouns, and clitics need their own
paradigm tracks. A singular noun paradigm therefore records the indefinite
subject, object, genitive/dative and ablative roles, plus the definite subject,
object and shared genitive/dative/ablative surface. Count-noun paradigms also
keep the indefinite and definite plural, the shared definite
genitive/dative/ablative plural, and the distinct indefinite ablative plural
(-sh/-ish) together. This follows the Academy of Sciences' published noun
declension tables. When Albanian uses one surface for more than one job, the
data preserves that syncretism rather than inventing a different ending.

Every declared noun surface is eligible for Train even when it is uncommon in
the current story route. The scheduler completes the learner's least-mastered
layer before repeating a form; story frequency only breaks ties within that
layer. A wrong answer receives its exact role refresher and remains unmastered,
so failure cannot silently advance the paradigm. `nounparadigmaudit.mjs`
enforces role completeness and `formprogressionaudit.mjs` proves every reviewed
surface is reachable without starvation. When two grammatical roles have the
same spelling, the role table keeps both but a context-free spelling question
shows that surface only once; pretending the learner could distinguish two
identical answers without sentence context would be an invalid quiz.

## Comprehension safety

The achievement gate never constructs an English sentence by concatenating
word glosses. It asks one of two explicitly labelled question types:

- a sentence question drawn only from an authored whole-line reading or an
  exact displayed source quotation with a registered game translation; or
- an isolated-word question drawn from an unambiguous, concrete word the
  player encountered on that achievement's path.

Context-sensitive particles, pronouns, articles, unresolved slash glosses, and
ambiguous Albanian surface forms are excluded from isolated-word questions.
Distractors are real English readings or concise dictionary meanings; the old
word-swapping distractor generator has been removed.

Every static player action now shows a natural full-phrase English reading and
keeps its token glosses in a separate row labelled **Word by word**. The two
layers are deliberately not merged: the action reading explains what the
choice means, while the token row still teaches how its Albanian parts align.
The audit refuses publication if an option, item-use phrase, or healing phrase
is missing from the address-and-Albanian-pinned action registry, remains marked
as a candidate, or no longer matches the live game text. The generated item
distractor has only one constrained grammatical form per eligible item, and
all 12 possible renderings are exercised by the audit. Identical Albanian
actions must also keep identical English across registry files. The audit pins
two narrow contextual exceptions: *rrugës* can be a road or a village lane,
and *diellin* can name either the physical sun or the personified Sun.

The audit simulates 50 salted attempts for every one of the 145
achievements: 7,250 attempts and 29,000 questions. It requires four questions
per attempt, three unique options per question, the correct option to be
present, and every sentence answer to resolve to approved whole-line metadata.
With full reading coverage, all 29,000 simulated questions are sentence-based.
It also guards the reported regressions “You beautiful are,” “His mother stays
and wait,” and singular “The dress have power.”

## Residual review boundary

Mechanical checks can prove provenance, metadata completeness, and that unsafe
fallback sentences do not enter an assessment. They cannot prove fluent or
idiomatic Albanian, faithful nuance, dialect choices, register, or literary
quality with 100% certainty.

The internal editorial campaign has completed the tale-by-tale whole-line pass
and an independent second pass across the reading tranches. Further assurance
should proceed as follows:

1. Compare each scene against its cited source in the
   [folklore source index](albanian-folklore.md) and the local
   [reference library](references/README.md).
2. Preserve every `Q()` source quotation. Do not silently standardize or
   “improve” the historical/dialectal witness.
3. Have a fluent Albanian reviewer correct only game-authored Albanian prose,
   recording disagreements and dialect decisions.
4. Keep every registry line pinned to its exact Albanian rendering; a changed
   line must fail the audit until its reading is reviewed again.
5. Keep reviewed action English distinct from the labelled word-by-word token
   row, and review any newly authored action before updating the registry seal.
6. Re-run the language audit, quote checker, story audit, and production build
   after every completed tale.

The repository now meets the zero-fallback and internal-review requirements.
It still must not describe internal model/editor review as native-speaker
approval: that final sociolinguistic assurance requires a fluent Albanian human
reviewer, particularly for dialect, register and idiom.
